# HC027H Focused Playtest

## Fast path
Use **Shift+8**. Target **60–90 seconds**.

## First 20 seconds
Do not immediately interact. Orbit/look and answer:
- Can I notice more than one receptor?
- Do at least two feel different?
- Does one catch my attention for a reason other than “it is closest”?

## First choice
Approach whichever receptor you prefer. There should be no visible route ring telling you which one is correct. Interact/read when you naturally reach it.

## After first read
Check whether the other two still feel like valid questions rather than leftover checklist items.

## Grade
- **A:** you form a preference naturally and can describe why.
- **B:** you notice options but choose mostly by convenience/distance.
- **C:** all three look interchangeable.
- **D:** you still perceive one obvious authored target.

Do not judge HC027I verb-affordance yet; HC027H is only proving player intention before instruction.

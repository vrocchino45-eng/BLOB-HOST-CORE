## Physical finding: reject the additive pressure offset

The existing Vascular owner computes a directional current plus separate turbulence swirl. The movement controller then couples that flow using pressure-dependent factors:

- Environmental acceleration includes (0.38 + pressure × 0.70), using the inspected onboarding tuning.
- The flow-velocity target includes (0.72 + pressure × 1.28).

The original candidate simultaneously reduced directional flow by 3% and added +0.03 pressure. The pressure increase counteracted most of the intended local-flow consequence. With a quiet fixture at pressure 0.72, raw flow was 3% lower but environmental acceleration only 0.70% lower and the flow-velocity target only 0.73% lower. Under full Stabilize, the acceleration difference fell to about 0.45%.

Selected candidate: keep the existing local pressure value exactly unchanged and multiply only the existing directional flow-strength component by 1 − 0.03 × Host amplitude. Do not scale the entire flow vector, because that would also modify swirl. Do not change viscosity, speed, acceleration tuning, boundaries, valve eligibility or verb permission.

The total-vector difference is smaller with swirl because swirl is deliberately unchanged. This does not weaken or reverse the directional component. These numbers compare isolated actual Vascular-method output and source-verified movement coupling scalars; they are not full movement trajectories or human perception measurements.

This is an authored prototype representation of external predatory pressure, not simulated predatory-host AI. The repeated episode represents recurring outside pressure; it is not a reward, debuff unlock or exact beat window. The numbers are a candidate established for the next implementation pass, still subject to actual rendering/feel evidence.

It does not guarantee a player staying briefly will see it, or establish that 3% alone is perceptible. Visual evidence is required before acceptance.

The clock advances through the existing runtime substeps. It is not browser wall time. No new pause or clock policy is introduced.

# HC027E Focused Playtest — Consequence Rhythm / System Turn-Taking

Target duration: **5–8 minutes**.

Keep the HC027D1 listening level where it already felt good. Do not raise the system volume just for this pass.

## A. Audio regression — 30 seconds
- Confirm overall loudness feels the same as HC027D1.
- Pulse/Absorb/Stabilize should not suddenly be globally louder or quieter.

If the whole mix changed, treat that as a regression.

## B. Core Work hierarchy — 1–2 minutes
1. Enter close Core inspection/Core Work.
2. Select an anatomy but do not grab it yet.
3. Grab/hold an anatomy and move it through radial space.
4. Place it.

Look for:
- selected/held anatomy becoming the obvious first read
- surrounding Host material stepping back noticeably more than HC027D1
- Guide/Tender still readable but not competing with the piece
- environment returning after the manipulation resolves

Report specifically if the close view becomes **too empty/dim** or if it is **still cluttered**.

## C. Pulse answer rhythm — about 1 minute
1. Pulse toward something close.
2. Pulse toward something farther away.
3. Watch/listen through emission → contact → return.

After the **return**, the world response should remain readable for roughly another second instead of vanishing immediately.

Question: does the return now feel like an answer you can register before deciding what to do next?

## D. Absorb + Stabilize resolution — about 1 minute
- Absorb a real target and watch the moment of capture.
- Use Stabilize long enough to reach lock.

Expected:
- capture gets a short metabolic afterflow beat
- Stabilize lock gets a short coherence afterglow
- neither should freeze controls or become a cinematic pause

## E. Quiet traversal — 2–3 minutes
Move through a quieter stretch without repeatedly firing verbs.

After a few seconds, existing Host material should become **subtly easier to notice**.

It should NOT feel like:
- a waypoint turning on
- an objective prompt
- a forced encounter
- the game yelling where to go

It should feel more like the living environment gradually re-entering your attention.

## Main playtest question
Does the existing game now read more like:

**act → answer → brief consequence → breathing room → world becomes noticeable again**

instead of:

**everything at once** or **nothing happening for too long**?

A short video is useful if the answer is hard to describe visually, but written feedback is enough if the difference is obvious.

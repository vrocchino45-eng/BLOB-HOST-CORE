# HC-028H2 Focused Playtest

Use the **same region and same target types** while changing only Core Work topology.

1. Put all three pieces Deep. Use Pulse, Absorb, Stabilize for ~45 seconds.
2. Put all three pieces Membrane. Repeat the same actions.
3. Try all Inner, then 1:1:1.
4. Ignore HUD unless something feels wrong.

## Acceptance
- Deep verbs feel heavier and leave longer visual/audio tails without becoming stronger merely because of H2.
- Membrane verbs feel sharper/quicker and more exposed without gaining H2 range or power.
- Inner reads cleaner/more controlled.
- 1:1:1 stays broadly neutral/adaptive.
- Pulse / Absorb / Stabilize remain immediately recognizable as the same three verbs across every topology.
- No change to Hub selection, route completion, movement controls, H1 environmental coupling, or 3→6→12 timing.

## Fail Conditions
- One topology is obviously the "best" because H2 changes gameplay power.
- Audio becomes substantially louder than the HC027D1 reference.
- VFX character makes a verb hard to identify.
- H1 environmental handling or HC028G lift-field readability regresses.

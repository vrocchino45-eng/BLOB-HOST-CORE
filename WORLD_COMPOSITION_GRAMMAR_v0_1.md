# BLOB — World Composition Grammar v0.1

## Purpose
Define a reusable world-authoring language that composes existing BLOB laws instead of requiring a new mechanic for every area.

## Core rule
A region is not only geometry or content. It is a composition of spatial, temporal, tonal, material, force, ecological, and scale relationships.

**World Signature = Key + Meter + Tempo + Octave + Matter + Force + Geometry + Ecology**

Only two or three dimensions should dominate the player's immediate read. The rest support them.

## Fundamental triad
The Core's three primary functions are the tonal roots of the language:

- **P — Pulse:** outward expression, excitation, transmission
- **A — Absorb:** inward reception, integration, compression
- **S — Stabilize:** coherence, sustain, anchoring, relation

These are not literal Western notes. They are functional tonal identities that audio may express through ratios.

## 3 → 6 → 12 derivation
### 3 roots
P / A / S

### 6 relational keys
The three roots can be ordered six ways:
- P-S-A
- P-A-S
- A-S-P
- A-P-S
- S-P-A
- S-A-P

The order describes dominant → secondary → tertiary world behavior.

### 12 counterpart modes
Long-term direction: each relational key may have an A-strand and B-strand/counterpart interpretation, producing twelve modes without inventing twelve unrelated systems.

This counterpart layer is design direction only in HC028C; it is not implemented.

## Temporal dimensions
- **Meter:** how a place organizes time.
- **Tempo:** how fast the place lives.
- **Phase:** where the place currently sits inside its cycle.
- **Polyrhythm:** two or more world processes sharing space while following different subdivisions of a larger common clock.

## Octave / scale
Octave means recurrence of the same relational law at another magnitude, not merely pitch. A microscopic rhythm may reappear as an organ-scale, host-scale, or cosmological process with a different temporal duration and spatial expression.

## Composition constraint
Before creating a new mechanic, first ask whether the intended experience can be produced by recombining an existing:
- key,
- meter,
- tempo,
- octave,
- matter state,
- force relationship,
- geometry,
- ecology.

Novel mechanics should be reserved for truths that cannot be expressed through the existing grammar.

## HC028C prototype — Vascular
Vascular is the first explicit composition test:

- **Key:** P-S-A
- **Meter:** 4/4
- **Tempo:** ~82.8 BPM
- **Clock relation:** two 4/4 bars inside the existing 5.8-second HC022F Host breath
- **Octave:** micro-local
- **Beat function phrase:** Pulse → Stabilize → Absorb → Stabilize
- **Physical expression:** existing current strength subtly breathes with the beat, capped to 0.96–1.06 of its pre-HC028C value
- **Visual expression:** vessel, wall folds, moving current motes, and charge share the beat
- **Audio expression:** restrained low three-function tones on the existing ambience bus

## Authority rules
The composition grammar must not become a disguised rhythm-game gate by default.

For HC028C:
- no beat-timed input requirement,
- no beat-timed route completion,
- no new objective,
- no new progression authority,
- no new geometry,
- no independent Vascular timer,
- no change to HC027D1 mix calibration.

The player should first *feel* the world's meter before the design ever asks them to reason about it.

## Future authoring direction
If the Vascular prototype feels coherent rather than mechanical, future regions can receive authored signatures. Candidate dimensions include:
- Silt: pressure/compression-biased key and heavier subdivision
- Grave: slow retained-memory timing with delayed resolution
- Hub: tonic/balanced reference state
- Act 3: displaced accents, counterpart modes, prime/asymmetric meters
- Act 4: multiple apparently incompatible signatures resolving into larger common cycles

Exact assignments are intentionally not locked by v0.1.


## HC028C2 calibration — accent is not force
The first HC028C video established that Vascular's single shared clock improved coherence but did not yet make **4/4 grouping** perceptually distinct. HC028C2 therefore separates two layers:

- **Perceptual accent:** `[1.00, 0.28, 0.64, 0.22]`
- **Movement accent:** retained HC028C `[1.00, 0.42, 0.68, 0.46]` with the same 0.96–1.06 flow bounds

The four-beat phrase is now authored as:
1. Pulse — transmit/downbeat
2. Stabilize — catch/hold
3. Absorb — receive/draw
4. Stabilize — settle/prepare

This establishes a composition rule: **a world's musical readability may become stronger without making its physics more coercive.** Meter should first be perceived through coordinated material, light, motion and restrained audio articulation; timing authority remains a separate design choice.

## Core engine relation to world grammar
The Core's own 3→6→12 engine is now presented as a tri-axial gyroscopic firing system rather than a flat polygon. This makes the Core itself a moving three-function oscillator capable of visually gaining coherence or precession while remaining orientation-independent. It is a potential future bridge between Core rhythm and world rhythm, but HC028C2 does not yet synchronize Core firing to Vascular meter or give phase architecture gameplay authority.

## HC-028D — Core ↔ World Resonance Layer

World Composition now has a first explicit bridge to the Core engine. This does **not** make BLOB a rhythm game and does not give alignment a hidden stat bonus.

### Causal relationship

`World Signature → Host rhythm/composition → Core/world resonance → presentation`

The bridge reads:
- regional key / meter / tempo / octave / current accented function
- Core 3→6→12 architecture / current firing function / cycle phase

It publishes a relationship state:
- `aligned`
- `resolving`
- `oscillating`
- `strained`
- `opposed`

### Foundation rule

Resonance is initially **descriptive, not authoritative**. It may alter gyroscopic readability and restrained world response, but it may not retime the Core engine, change movement, change route success, gate verbs, or require beat-perfect input.

### Vascular proof

Vascular remains `P–S–A / 4/4 / ~82.8 BPM / micro-local octave`. The Core engine can now visibly entrain with the world phrase: the currently emphasized world function subtly strengthens the corresponding gyroscopic plane, coherent alignment reduces apparent precession, and Vascular tissue provides a small reply.

This creates the first vertical connection between **regional composition** and **Core physiology** while keeping both original owners intact.

### Next intended bridge

HC-028D1 may let Pulse / Absorb / Stabilize visibly route through their corresponding gyroscopic systems. That is explicitly outside HC-028D so Core↔World resonance can be judged on its own.

## HC-028D1 — Core ↔ Verb Engine Coupling

The Core's three verbs now visibly originate from the same triadic engine that defines its 3→6→12 architecture. This adds a second vertical causal bridge:

`player intent → verb-engine coupling → gyroscopic function plane → existing external verb expression`

- **Pulse:** outward route through the Pulse plane.
- **Absorb:** inward route through the Absorb plane.
- **Stabilize:** coherence route through the Stabilize plane plus reduced whole-engine precession.

Existing simultaneous input can appear as relationships without becoming new abilities:
- Pulse + Absorb → `exchange`
- Stabilize + Pulse → `cohere_project`
- Stabilize + Absorb → `cohere_draw`
- all three → `full_system`

These labels are diagnostic grammar, not player-facing combo names or stat states. HC028D1 does not modify verb power, timing, range, cost, movement, targeting, progression, world rhythm, or route completion.

This establishes an important composition rule: **the Core should not merely emit abilities; its visible internal physiology should explain how those abilities are being produced.**

## HC-028D2 — Deliberate commitment punctuation

The three Core verbs are the physical language; A/E is punctuation, not a fourth generic verb. If movement, Pulse, Absorb, Stabilize, or their relationships can express the outcome, A/E is not required. A/E is reserved for authored choices that make a relationship meaningful or persistent. Core Work grab/place is a valid exception because it is an intentional internal arrangement.

Resolved traversal is cadence: when a route has truly resolved, the world opens/returns through spatial state rather than requiring an extra confirmation press.

## HC-028E — Regional Composition Triad

HC-028E proves that the same Core/control grammar can inhabit three perceptually different regional sentences without adding buttons, clocks, or route rules.

All three regions remain inside the same **5.8-second Host cycle**:

| Region | Key | Meter | Host-cycle parsing | Subdivision tempo | Dominant feel |
|---|---|---|---|---:|---|
| Vascular | P-S-A | 4/4 | 2 bars × 4 | ~82.8 BPM | transmission / catch / receive / settle |
| Silt | A-S-P | 3/4 | 2 bars × 3 | ~62.1 BPM | gather / brace / pressure-read release |
| Grave | S-A-P | 12/8 | 1 bar × 12, grouped 3+3+3+3 | ~124.1 eighths/min | hold / draw / return / settle with memory echoes |

For Grave, the 124.1 value describes the **eighth-note subdivision**, not a fast perceived pulse. Its four triplet groups occur at roughly **41.4 compound groups per minute**, so it should feel heavy and spacious while retaining internal motion.

### Regional voices

The audio renderer now reads its restrained rhythm voice from the regional composition profile rather than hard-coding Vascular. This is not a soundtrack generator and does not imitate a specific band. The profiles establish original functional traits:

- **Vascular — transmission pulse:** clearer forward articulation and 4/4 downbeat hierarchy.
- **Silt — pressure bass triplet:** lower center, longer inward first beat, restrained brace, shorter release.
- **Grave — compound memory bass:** very low center, strong triplet-group starts, quiet inner echoes, delayed-feeling return articulation.

The HC027D1 reference mix remains the calibration authority. Regional identity comes from **rhythm, register, articulation and spacing**, not from raising master level.

### Resonance generalization

Core↔World resonance now accepts any authored regional composition profile. The Core therefore does not have a single preferred "Vascular rhythm"; it can become aligned, resolving, oscillating, strained, or opposed relative to whichever regional sentence is active.

Silt and Grave receive restrained region-side resonance replies, but the relationship remains descriptive in HC028E:

- no timed input success,
- no rhythm-derived route completion,
- no Silt/Grave movement multiplier,
- no verb power bonus,
- no new A-button requirement.

### Composition rule strengthened

**Different place does not require different controls.** Before adding a mechanic, attempt to create the difference by recomposing the same laws through key, meter, tempo, register, material response, force behavior and ecology.

## HC028G readability contract — verbs and force must declare themselves
HC028G adds no new composition authority. It establishes a presentation law for the existing grammar:

- **Pulse / expression:** the outgoing wave may be abstract, but matter that answers should briefly reveal its own structure. In the current implementation, responding chemistry geometry receives a temporary wireframe contour echo.
- **Absorb / integration:** transfer must visibly run from source matter toward the Core. Helical filaments and corkscrewing fragments communicate incorporation rather than generic disappearance.
- **Stabilize / coherence:** regulation should visibly move from disorder toward phase alignment. The target field uses gyroscopic bands whose wobble/precession collapses as existing Stabilize progress rises.
- **Environmental force:** invisible force should be inferred from visible matter consequence. HC028G reads the resolved PlayerMovementController environmental force and pressure; it does not invent a second thermal/updraft physics map.

This keeps the broader rule intact: **the player reads laws from matter, motion, sound and consequence before consulting instrumentation.**

## HC-028H — Core topology as an interpreter of world composition

Regional composition is no longer expressed against a single neutral Core presentation. Existing Core Work topology acts as an embodied interpreter: **response** controls how quickly a regional phrase appears in the Core, **inertia** how long its imprint persists, **coherence** how held the gyroscopic response appears, and **exposure** how directly the Host reaches the Core visually. These properties reuse existing topology laws and add no new clock, input gate, route authority or gameplay modifier in HC028H.


## HC-028I — Ecology enters the same sentence

The World Composition grammar now has a first authored ecology consumer:

`Host rhythm → regional physical phrase → Core/world resonance → organism entrainment`

HC028I does **not** assign creatures a new combat class or rhythm-game timing rule. It uses the two organisms that already exist in the runtime to establish the physical vocabulary:

- **Pulse / expression:** anatomy opens, broadcasts, flares or pushes attention outward.
- **Absorb / reception:** anatomy cups, gathers and draws attention inward.
- **Stabilize / coherence:** sensing/anchoring structures settle and reduce visible wobble.

The existing nursery Tender can carry this language into other regions only when its already-authored care bond has been earned. The membrane Watcher remains tied to the nursery. Core↔World resonance changes how strongly this shared phrase reads, but EntitySystem remains the authority for actual behavior, care, bonding and authored player reactions.

This establishes the ecology rule: **an organism should feel like another living participant in the same Host laws before it is treated as an enemy, quest giver, or mechanical object.**


## HC-028J — Organisms interpret chords, not buttons

HC028J extends the ecology sentence from regional entrainment into direct relational interpretation:

`world composition → Core state → P/A/S chord → organism-specific interpretation → body-language reply`

The chord remains universal; the **meaning is contextual to the organism**. A controlled inward gesture (`Stabilize + Absorb`) can read as safe receiving to a trusted care/filter organism while reading as contained sampling to an anchored sensing organism. `Pulse + Absorb` can read as reciprocal exchange to the Tender and counter-sensing to the Watcher.

This establishes a new ecology authoring rule:

> **Do not give each organism a bespoke interaction button. Give it a way of interpreting the same physical language.**

HC028J is intentionally non-authoritative: it cannot alter bond, repair, movement, combat, targeting, progression, route completion or verb strength. Persistent consequences may be authored later only after the relationship language itself proves readable.


## HC-028K — Reciprocal ecological memory

Entity relationship grammar now has short-lived temporal continuity. A meaningful Core gesture can leave a decaying embodied trace in an organism after the input ends, while the regional Host phrase continues underneath it.

This establishes a useful distinction:

`world phrase → current entity interpretation → short-lived organism memory`

Memory is **not affinity/progression**. It does not award relationship points or unlock content. It allows ecology to retain recent physical history so an organism does not behave as though every frame is its first encounter with the Core.

The current proof remains deliberately limited to the authored Tender and Watcher.

## HC-028L — Reciprocal ecological behavior

HC028K established temporary organism memory. HC028L lets that memory bias the organism's next authored behavior without introducing relationship points or a new AI authority.

Causal chain:

`regional composition → Core chord → organism interpretation → retained memory → bounded next behavior`

- **Tender:** retained safe/reciprocal memory can bring the already-bonded companion slightly closer; guarded/testing memory can increase protective spacing; settled memory can soften follow response. Actual repair need remains dominant and repair rates do not change.
- **Watcher:** retained sample/counter/defensive memory changes attention tracking and scan behavior while the organism remains physically anchored.
- **Architecture:** `EntityReciprocalBehaviorSystem` publishes directives only. `EntitySystem` remains the movement/behavior owner.
- **Forbidden:** hidden affinity score, dialogue-tree state, combat conversion, new targeting, route guidance, bond/repair multiplier, player-movement effect, timing gate.

The design goal is reciprocity: the player should be able to perceive that an organism not only *saw* the Core's relational gesture, but briefly **acts differently because it remembers it**.

## HC028M — Reciprocal ecology / local matter rule

Ecology may now participate in the composition chain as a **subordinate local matter voice**:

`Core relationship → organism interpretation → short-lived memory → organism behavior → local Host-matter response`

The organism does not replace the region's key/meter/matter law. It publishes only a bounded local signature, and the existing regional/Host material owner combines that signature with the current place. Therefore a Tender care response in Vascular should still read as Vascular matter, while the same relationship in Silt or Grave is physically interpreted by those regions.

**Locked law:** organism-local ecology may shape nearby existing matter presentation, but may not add a second clock, new particle field, player force, collision rule, chemistry result, route gate, objective, affinity score or progression authority.
## HC028M1 — ecological consequence readability

Ecological causality is now presented as a temporal sentence rather than one simultaneous effect stack:

**Core relationship → organism interpretation → retained memory/behavior → local Host-matter answer → regional decay.**

The local matter clause uses only the existing Host motes and remains subordinate to regional composition. During the live Core chord, matter stays quiet enough for organism anatomy to own the immediate read. After input release, a bounded local pocket answers the organism, sustains through retained memory, then dissolves back into the current region. This timing is perceptual grammar, not a rhythm/input gate.

Camera readability may temporarily protect the Core→organism sight corridor through the existing fade/pullback owner, but ecology does not own camera mode, targeting or movement.

## HC029A Symbol-Language Lock — Relationship Geometry

The first production iconography is **grammar, not HUD signage**.

- **Pulse:** triangle apex resolves outward along the Core→target relationship axis. Static shorthand may be drawn as `▼`, but runtime orientation is relative to the relationship, not permanent screen-down.
- **Absorb:** the opposed triangle resolves inward along target→Core. Static shorthand may be drawn as `▲`, but runtime orientation remains relationship-relative.
- **Stabilize:** an enclosing coherence boundary. Its vibration, displaced center and asymmetry communicate how much disagreement/instability the Core is attempting to reconcile.
- **Closure:** completion of a directional triangle or boundary communicates operation completion/coherence rather than a progress bar.
- **Symmetry / phase-lock:** increasing symmetry and synchronized motion communicate successful stabilization.
- **Asymmetry / oscillation:** strain, disagreement and instability.
- **3→6→12 remains Core resolution**, not a prohibition on world geometry. The Host may present squares, hexagons, octagons, irregular forms and later prime/incompatible structures.
- **Chords compose primitives:** P+S may show outward direction within a coherence boundary; A+S inward direction within coherence; P+A opposed directional forms; P+A+S a full composed Core sentence. Do not invent unrelated icons where the primitive grammar can compose the meaning.

The Core, Host tissue, organisms, residue and region architecture should eventually express the same alphabet in different physical dialects.


## Rupture consequence / retained matter
At higher octave resolution, rupture is not a disposable combat effect. Material that is harvested leaves the local field; material that is missed remains as a bounded regional memory. Its influence is strongest while fresh and settles to a weaker persistent trace. Different material families bias different parts of the region's existing resonance grammar. This is consequence, not a mandatory cleanup objective.

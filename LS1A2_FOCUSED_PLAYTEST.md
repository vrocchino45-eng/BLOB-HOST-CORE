# LS1A2 — Natural roaming test (6–10 minutes)

Start in Silt in the **LS1A2 patched** build. Use the same controller mapping; you do not need to complete the route immediately.

**0–2 minutes — Pressure throat:** Wander along the original ribs. Does the spine now feel stone-lined and enclosed without swallowing the Core/Guide in the foreground? Are there large recognizable rock/silt masses rather than floating unrelated props?

**2–5 minutes — Reservoir and side habitats:** Enter the reservoir, garden and veins. Check that every apparent seep mouth is passable, with no stone polygons stretched across an opening. Compare the dense porous throats to the hollow relief of each cavity. Does everything feel like one connected body instead of separate hanging spheres?

**5–7 minutes — Retest original interaction:** Establish the pressure relationship with Stabilize then Pulse; brace ribs; Absorb the yellow porous packet (not dark packed residue); visit the deep release and return if convenient. Confirm original Guide prompts remain usable.

**7–10 minutes — Return and fatigue check:** Wander back through a lateral branch. Watch whether stone/silt geometry obscures the camera or creates strange visible outside-the-world holes. Check framerate at first Silt load and after rotating camera in garden/veins.

**Report:** Most helpful observations: “felt like a cavern”, “still floating in open space”, “could pass through apparent solid rock”, “opening looked blocked”, “Core disappeared behind wall”, “direction confusing”, “pressure sequence broke.” A short clip (under 10 minutes) is plenty. No need for a 20-minute run until renewable material exists.

**Out of scope:** Replenishing yellow packet, settling/transported fragments, agents, lasting erosion. Those belong to LS1B/LS1C. Do not judge LS1A2 as a mature 20-minute sandbox.

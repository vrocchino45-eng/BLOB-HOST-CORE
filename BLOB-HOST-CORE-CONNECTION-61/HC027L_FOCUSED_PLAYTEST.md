Use **F1 Playtest Trace** for the recording and make the captured game window fill as much of the video frame as possible.

## Preferred test
Run a normal continuous play session for about **8 minutes**. Do not deliberately rush to trigger every system. Play according to what catches your attention. Use Shift+1..8 only if the continuous path stalls or if you need to revisit a specific condition.

## What the trace now tells us

## Acceptance questions
1. Can you orient without immediately reading instructions?
2. Does something in the world create voluntary curiosity?
3. Does the chosen verb feel like a choice rather than a prescribed key?
4. After an interaction, do you get enough time to perceive the consequence before another layer competes?
5. Does the screen visibly re-open to the Host after that consequence?
6. When Pulse answers, can you distinguish local/reachable evidence from deep structural evidence?
7. Does HC027K make another condition interesting without feeling like a waypoint?
8. During quiet travel, does spatial understanding keep changing, or does it become empty travel?
9. Do charged Pulse / sustained Absorb / sustained Stabilize remain visually readable without HUD dependence?
10. Can Deep / Inner / Membrane operating personality be felt when committed verbs are used?

## Rhythm target
Quiet observation counts. Empty movement does not. The run should rarely spend 60–90 seconds without at least one of: a meaningful decision, a new observation, an active consequence, or a change in spatial understanding.

## Do not optimize during the run
Do not intentionally press every verb just to prove it works. We already have system validators. This playtest is for whether the current systems form a sustained game rhythm.

# HC027K Focused Playtest — Consequence Creates Next Question

## Primary test
Use **Shift+8 — Signal Plain**.

Spend about 3–5 minutes in the area. The goal is not to complete the old receptor loop quickly. Test one successful local consequence at a time and watch what happens elsewhere.

### Test A — Absorb relationship
1. Find the slow draw/release receptor.
2. Use sustained Absorb until its persistent redistributed-flow state clearly establishes.
3. Release the verb and stop moving for a moment.
4. Look across the local volume rather than at the receptor you just changed.

**Question:** does the afterflow seem to run into another unstable condition, making the phase-slip receptor newly interesting without an arrow telling you to go there?

### Test B — Pulse relationship
1. Find the call/wait receptor.
2. Use the local Pulse experiment that establishes coherent answer cadence.
3. Let the original response settle.
4. Watch/listen for another existing rhythm beginning to answer sympathetically.

**Question:** do you notice the slow breathing receptor differently because the cadence affected it?

### Test C — Stabilize relationship
1. Find the phase-slip receptor.
2. Hold Stabilize until its local coherent pocket persists.
3. Release and observe the broader field.

**Question:** does the stable pocket make the resonant call/wait gap elsewhere easier to perceive?

## Acceptance test
With the debug HUD hidden, a successful interaction should support this thought sequence:

> “I changed this.”
>
> “Something else over there is behaving differently because of it.”
>
> “I want to see what that relationship means.”

That is a pass even if you choose not to approach the newly affected receptor.

## Failure signs
- It looks like a waypoint or glowing route line.
- The target receptor is merely brighter rather than behaviorally different.
- The source consequence and target change happen simultaneously and read as one noisy effect.
- Several relationships compete at equal visual strength.
- You can only identify the relationship by reading debug text.
- The new cue feels mandatory or implies a correct receptor order.

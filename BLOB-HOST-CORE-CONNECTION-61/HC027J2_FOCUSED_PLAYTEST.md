# HC027J2 Focused Playtest

Use **Shift+8** for the verb/world read, then **Shift+5** for radial-physiology comparison.

## A. Pulse commitment
With the HUD hidden, compare a quick X release with an approximately one-second hold/release. Quick Pulse must still feel local and clean. Charged Pulse should feel more expensive and structurally deeper. After a committed release, stop input and look for readable Core recovery rather than a cooldown UI.

## B. Deep versus Membrane Pulse
At the physiology test platform, use Core Work to bias the three retained pieces toward Deep, test charged Pulse, then bias them toward Membrane and repeat. Deep should take longer to build but recover comfortably. Membrane should reach commitment faster and answer farther/more aggressively, but consume visibly more reserve and leave more sensory ringing.

## C. Sustained Absorb
Hold Absorb while moving. The draw should strengthen as commitment develops and movement should become progressively less free. Membrane-heavy should feel aggressive but costly; Deep-heavy should feel slower/steadier and sustainable. Release Absorb: ordinary movement should immediately stop being penalized.

## D. Sustained Stabilize
Hold Stabilize around the Signal Plain phase-slip condition. The Core should become increasingly committed and movement should feel anchored. Deep-heavy should sustain a strong coherent pocket; Membrane-heavy should require noticeably more effort and produce weaker sustained coherence.

## E. Acceptance
The test passes if, without reading numbers, you can feel that:
1. quick versus committed use is a choice rather than a cosmetic variant;
2. Deep feels like reserve/stability, Membrane like output/consumption, and Inner like control;
3. committed verbs visibly cost the Core something;
4. normal traversal is not slowed when no verb is being committed;
5. quick Pulse remains trustworthy local/reachable information.

Turn the debug HUD back on only to diagnose an unclear result. It now exposes active verb commitment, output/coherence, movement multiplier and reserve.

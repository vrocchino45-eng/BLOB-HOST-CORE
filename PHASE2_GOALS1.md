## Silt II: conserved grain round trip

First Silt taught pressure, selective appetite and release of the passage. Silt II uses the already accepted material interaction as an explicit, accountable round trip.

1. Grip one existing grain after phase-two eligibility.
2. Bring that same grain into the garden patch and Pulse through real braced contact until it imprints.
3. Deliberately seat the grain.
4. Extract that grain.
5. Move clear of the receiving contact and deliberately release it.
6. Allow the existing trajectory attractor to return that same free grain near its native path.

Every proof belongs to the same grain ID and attempt. Pulse and Absorb retain their current movement roles; Absorb is available when useful and is not an artificial mandatory button check. No material controls, targeting ranges, tether limits, pressure threshold or native-attractor physics change.

The goal observes actual route receipts: acquired grip, real imprint, plate-seated, plate-extracted, qualified deliberate free release, then that unit's nearNative state on a subsequent mounted trajectory advance. It snapshots the transaction cursor when eligibility begins, so a loop performed during the first visit cannot be credited retroactively.

The final step uses the existing per-unit nearNative signal, not the trajectory module's global lastEvent. Existing thresholds remain: the attractor enters near-native below 0.22 local units and leaves above 0.32. No extra dwell timer or exact-speed requirement is added. Native return may happen immediately on the first valid advance if the player released close to the path; that is a valid spatial solution.

A simple drop cannot finish the goal. The original porous packet, autonomous growth, wave finishes, a different grain, a seat without imprint, release caused by region hiding, or another grain's near-native event cannot satisfy the sequence. A region exit cannot finish the journey while hidden. On re-entry, a later genuine deliberate release and matching native return are still required if the previous release was implicit.

The attempt stores a bounded current grain ID, accepted stage and sequence references. Choosing another grain before seating starts a new unfinished attempt rather than combining proofs. After a grain is seated, its extraction/release proof must follow that seated ID. Existing material/seat ownership remains authoritative. All eight grains and their mass remain conserved.

A completed round trip awards silt:phase_2 once. Normal material play continues afterward.

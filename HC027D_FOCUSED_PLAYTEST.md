# HC027D Focused Playtest — 2 to 4 Minutes

Use a normal external Chrome/Edge window. Click the AUDIO LOCKED banner once and verify the unlock triad before testing.

## 1. Pulse distance
- Stand near a visible chemistry object.
- Press **X** once.
- Watch the outgoing wave reach it and the answer return.
- Move farther away and repeat.
- Expected: farther material answers later.

## 2. Boundary vs openness
- Pulse near an obvious Host boundary.
- Move into a more open pocket and Pulse again.
- Expected: response density/timing changes; open space is allowed to be comparatively quiet.

## 3. Deep vs Membrane physiology
In Core Work, compare a strongly Deep layout with a strongly Membrane layout against similar nearby material.

Expected:
- Deep = lower / slower / longer
- Membrane = brighter / quicker / sharper
- range remains the same

## 4. Absorb process
- Hold **RT** near a valid target.
- Listen for intake → flow → capture.
- Hold RT with no useful target nearby.
- Expected: empty draw reads differently from successful material flow.

## 5. Stabilize process
- Hold **LT** on a readable target.
- Expected: separated tones converge as order builds and reinforce near lock.

## 6. Mix check
Do not raise system output to maximum first. Confirm whether the new 0.68 master level is comfortably readable at your normal listening setting.

## What to report
- Does Pulse feel like a wave returning information, or just delayed sound effects?
- Can you sense distance from the delay without watching the HUD?
- Is Deep vs Membrane obvious enough?
- Does Absorb tell you whether it is actually pulling something?
- Does Stabilize feel like disorder becoming ordered?
- Is anything irritating, too repetitive, or still too quiet?

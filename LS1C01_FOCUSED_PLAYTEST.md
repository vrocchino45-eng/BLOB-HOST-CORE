# LS1C0.1 — Focused User Playtest

**Target: 6–9 minutes**, rather than a 20-minute recording.

1. **Silt:** Enable Quick Look F4 / hold LB+RB. Observe whether Core label follows Core while you fly and camera rotates; no label should float detached at an old screen coordinate.
2. **Reservoir:** Approach the original yellow porous packet. Its bubble should identify it as a one-time packet and display RT. After absorption, the original packet should not falsely reappear in the HUD; a separate replenishing stock may be labeled once its material cycle is active.
3. **Garden / Veins:** Move into their existing anatomical pockets and look toward their center. The habitat name and pertinent current-cycle counts should appear *only while the corresponding scene anchor is visible/in range*. On leaving a habitat, it should no longer act as a persistent screen card.
4. **Targeting:** Acquire a genuine existing canonical target. It may receive an object bubble if the target system exposes a scene root. Do not expect labels for arbitrary non-targeted stone.
5. **Different region:** Exit to Hub or Grave. Silt labels must disappear; generic Core/selected target presentation should continue.
6. **Report:** Click DESIGN NOTES or press Shift+F4 while Quick Look is open. Original verified controls, runtime counts, 3→6→12 Core status, and planned-vs-implemented caveats remain accessible. Close with F4.
7. **Feel:** Is muting comfortable? Do 2–3 bubbles help or obstruct? Does the Core bubble get in the way while flying? Are labels legible enough at normal camera distance?

**Report with screenshots or a short video** showing a Core move/rotation, one packet interaction, and Garden/Veins navigation if possible. Note whether the bubbles correctly stick to things, and any bubble that appears to be associated with the wrong object.

**Do not expect yet:** full-world automated landmark recognition, rendered arrows to hidden spaces, ecological event popups, full controller deep-note navigation, or normal-HUD migration. Those are future evidence-dependent passes.

# HC-028F1 Focused Playtest — Hub Deliberate Route Commit

## Purpose
Confirm that the Hub now behaves as an orientation and destination-choice space rather than an activation puzzle.

## Test 1 — First Hub arrival → Grave
1. Enter Hub normally from Guide.
2. Do **not** Pulse every route and do not circle the center.
3. Fly toward the awake Grave Memory body.
4. Watch for the body to brighten/breathe more strongly when within the commit zone.
5. Press **A** once.

**Pass:** A expresses a deliberate Grave destination choice; the Core is pulled a short distance into the route mouth and Grave gameplay takes over. No Pulse, Status page, three-route scan, or center return is required merely to leave Hub.

## Test 2 — Grave return → Vascular / Silt
1. Complete Grave and follow its resolved return fold into Hub.
2. Approach either newly awake route body.
3. Press A when that destination wakes.

**Pass:** Hub does not ask for another hidden activation. A selects the chosen awake destination; regional gameplay begins after the short guided approach.

## Test 3 — Revisit truthfulness
After returning from Vascular or Silt, approach a route you have already entered and press A again.

**Pass:** if the Hub offers A, the route actually accepts it. No false selectable state is allowed.

## Test 4 — All three returns → Comb
After Grave, Vascular and Silt have all genuinely completed and returned, remain in Hub.

**Pass:** the returned conditions resolve into the Combward selector automatically. The old mandatory Hub spiral / inward compression / final Pulse is not required. Approach Comb and press A to commit.

## Feel target
Hub should feel like:

`arrive → orient → approach a living destination → commit → guided handoff → play the region`

It should **not** feel like:

`arrive → inspect HUD → Pulse several things → search center → circle trigger → press another verb → finally leave`

## Regression checks
- Region mechanics after entry are unchanged.
- Route returns remain proximity-resolved.
- Pulse/Absorb/Stabilize still work normally inside regions.
- A outside a valid Hub destination remains reserved/inert under HC028D2 grammar.
- Guided approach is short and voluntary; it is not a teleport.
- Controller mapping is unchanged.

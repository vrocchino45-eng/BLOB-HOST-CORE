# HC-009E Acceptance Tests

## Automated

Run:

```bash
npm run validate:first-minute-agency-slice-lock
npm run validate:current
npm run build
```

The focused simulation must prove:

- a passive clean packet remains ecological activity but not player success;
- passive delivery cannot reveal the Fold or begin Core growth;
- Stabilize cannot identify or purify unread matter;
- Pulse followed by a successful intervention proves agency;
- the existing Fold remains hidden and inert before agency, then emerges without changed requirements;
- the living circuit never completes the Fold, absorbs the protected seed, or starts the morph directly;
- all eight established cross-room truths are required for the slice lock;
- dormant Signal Chorus state cannot shortcut the lock;
- the lock observer never mutates triggers, objectives, gates, routes, or abilities;
- cohesion rings and beads remain hidden;
- runtime budgets remain `15 global / 15 → 13 → 8 active-region / 20 dormant`.

## Live F1-hidden pass

1. Let the first clean packet reach the Tender without input.
2. Confirm the nursery remains alive, but the Listening Fold does not appear.
3. On the scar-touched packet, hold Stabilize without pulsing; confirm the packet remains unread and contaminated.
4. Pulse that packet, then Stabilize beside it through the filter window.
5. Confirm the Tender recovers and the Watcher visibly turns toward the emerging Listening Fold.
6. Complete the unchanged Fold sequence and absorb the protected seed.
7. Confirm the Binding Core body—not rings or meters—is the retained evidence.
8. Traverse Guide Threshold and confirm its pressure response recognizes the form.
9. In Hub Chamber, complete all three route reads, the memory read, and return to center.
10. Open Status and confirm the self-reading commits.
11. Confirm Grave Memory becomes crossable while Vascular Rhythm and Silt Pressure remain dormant.
12. Open F1 only after the run and verify all eight slice-lock criteria are complete.

## Failure criteria

Do not lock the slice if:

- passive ecology reveals the Fold or begins growth;
- Stabilize substitutes for Pulse;
- the required nursery choice is only understandable through F1;
- the Fold appears before the Watcher responds;
- the Fold's established interaction requirements change;
- the HUD reports completion from Signal Chorus compatibility state;
- rings or percentages become the primary growth evidence;
- any active-region budget or progression owner changes.

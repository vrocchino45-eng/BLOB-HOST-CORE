# Experience Inspector — WC01 v0.2

Compare player intent, selected object, actual contact and accepted outcome against the visible cue while using the normal gameplay camera. Check blocked connectors, Shelf cue hierarchy, native Vascular sequence versus C2 and Relay responses, and Hub route wording. Require controller footage and matching owner evidence. Verify readability in both World framings and relevant labels-off/context tests, and preserve HC027D1 audio calibration. Reject a cue that implies unaccepted contact, a false destination or a false retained result. Do not modify a trade's source.

## Design and Canon Gate

Compare the evidence to the approved player experience and documented meaning. If an expected experience or lore claim has no approval, record an RFI. Do not approve a new design or canon interpretation in an inspection.

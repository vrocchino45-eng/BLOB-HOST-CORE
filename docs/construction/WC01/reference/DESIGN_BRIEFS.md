# DESIGN_BRIEFS — WC01 v0.2

Options for review. No option below is approved or canonical. Reference the [RFI register](DESIGN_RFI_REGISTER.md) for authority status.

| Brief / gaps | Possible approaches and tradeoffs | Systems, repeatability and intended player experience |
|---|---|---|
| DB-01 · S06 | Keep F1 as a bounded experimental circuit, which protects a reproducible mechanic. Or define one world-side interaction within A0 after ownership and anatomy are approved, which gives broader relevance and adds contact and retained-state dependencies. | Core embodiment, targeting, Flow, territory, physiology and presentation. Compare repeatable bounded contact and release with a separate environmental action. Player should recognize whether the world or Core caused conduction. |
| DB-02 · V02, E01 | Use existing cadence, recovery, II and history for variation first. More response from existing Relays can clarify causality without hydraulics changes. An explicit Relay-to-current or Relay-to-grain coupling would add a new rule, downstream effects and accounting risk. | Vascular route, EntitySystem, Silt ecology, C2, controls and feedback. Compare neutral, braced, misread and revisit. Player can recover and recognize the Relay response independently of actual flow. |
| DB-03 · G02, G03 | Identify and present the documented Husk or bed role. If its role is still insufficient, propose a new local symbolic or mechanical use and seek the relevant design and canon approvals. A new meaning adds attribution and historical reconciliation work. | Grave I, Grave II, world art, progression and lore. Confirm first reading versus remount and separate current recovery from retained history. Player should know whether the landmark can be read, affected or protected. |
| DB-04 · O02 | Retain distinct local Scar behaviors and use existing evidence to clarify thematic continuity. Or define an explicit recurring condition with regional differences. Shared state raises provenance and mounting risks. | Surface ecology, Scar threshold, Grave, Host condition, Core recovery. Compare first approach, revisit and neutral pressure. Player should recognize both the local hazard and whether a prior action changed it. A shared biology claim requires canon approval. |
| DB-05 · A01, D01 | Deepen current, reachable Plain/Ridge/Gathering interactions through existing recurrence and memory. Or scope one new reachable area after design and authority review. Expansion raises route, asset, persistence and coverage cost. | Aperture, encounter owners, Comb/boundaries, dormant recipes and lore. Compare first visit, interruption, repeat and return. Player should find a second outcome without needing debug entry. Archive activation remains outside WC01. |
| DB-06 · C01 | Keep separate local witnesses with existing IDs and improve their association only if a traced discrepancy persists. Or propose a shared optional narrative readout after its priorities and lifecycle are approved. The latter raises HUD and shared interface costs. | C2 receipt ledger, Vascular application, Grave witness, B5-B reserve, territory/labels. Matched no-release, stale and RT-only controls. Player can distinguish live delivery from history across a revisit. |

For approval record the selected approach, alternatives considered, owner and affected runtime IDs, approved language if any, lifecycle on leave/revisit, locked behavior, acceptance evidence, authority reference, and date. Then update the owning blueprint before issuing any order.

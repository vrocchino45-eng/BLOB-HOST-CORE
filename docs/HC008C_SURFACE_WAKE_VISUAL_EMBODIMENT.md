# HC-008C — Surface Wake Visual Embodiment

## Outcome

Surface Wake now presents the existing nursery ecology through role-specific bodies, anatomy-based placement, and temporary interaction effects. This pass adds no gameplay content and changes no progression owner.

## Visual grammar

- The Stage-1 player remains the dominant Seed Core focal point with quieter idle rings.
- The Tender is a broad cupped guardian physically sheltering the protected seed.
- The Watcher uses one dominant tracking lens instead of an orbital-node silhouette.
- The Guide remains a small folded lantern and relocates to interpret the active relationship.
- The Listening Fold is an incomplete layered membrane embedded at the wounded side.
- The current is a cross-chamber stream with matter traveling in one direction.
- The scar is attached to the wounded wall and exposes warning effects only on contact.
- The signal thread stays quiet until communication makes it relevant.
- The three optional motes read as droplet, carried bead, and scar-edge fragment.

## Spatial zones

1. Awakening pocket
2. Current lane
3. Listening wall
4. Protected seed pocket
5. Wounded exit seam

These are presentation zones, not new routes or gates.

## Locked progression

- Listening Fold: `SurfaceWakeLayeredInteractionSystem`
- Protected seed absorption: `BondSystem`
- Scar-or-signal first morph: `LocalCellMorphSystem`

The existing timing thresholds, seed chemistry, destination, controls, and all later route states remain locked.

## Live acceptance

With HUD and F1 hidden, the player should be able to identify who protects, watches, and guides; where matter flows; what is wounded; what is protected; where communication occurs; and why the nursery eventually releases the core.

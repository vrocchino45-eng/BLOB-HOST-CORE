## Meaning

The Scar Flow Fold is where the first membrane nursery pocket reveals that the host's wounds are not only blockages. Some wounds have become pressure routes.

This is important for BLOB's human allegory: old injury can block, protect, distort, or redirect life. The player should learn that scar matter is not simply enemy matter. It is meaning under pressure.

BLOB: HOST CORE — Universal Physics and Organization Blueprint v1

Status: Seven design principles approved in discussion. Architecture reference, not implementation authorization. Existing calibrated runtime behavior and Wave 08 scope remain unchanged. UP identifiers are newly assigned for traceability.

UP-01 — Matter, energy and information

Matter provides substance; energy enables change; information describes organization, relationships and retained patterns. Coordination integrates these aspects rather than constituting a fourth conserved resource. Ordinary information is physically encoded; bounded BLOB-specific mechanisms may retain or propagate patterns beyond their original material structure. Define carriers, capacity, conditions, losses and limits. Information may be copied, corrupted or lost; it is not a conserved inventory quantity. Reconstruction requires compatible matter, energy and organizational capability.

UP-02 — Fundamental forces and fields

Conventional physical interactions coexist with a shared BLOB-specific field. Flow is its dynamic exchange and coupling manifestation; Memory is its persistent organizational manifestation. Silt, Vascular, Grave and Hub specialize without exclusive ownership of matter, energy, information or coordination. Relic interprets rather than automatically controls. Emergent mechanics such as friction, adhesion, pressure and resonance are not additional fundamental forces. No unlimited energy, instantaneous communication or unrestricted reconstruction. Field name, equations, propagation and constants remain open.

UP-03 — Physical scale and reference frames

Open-ended nested hierarchy with recurring organizational patterns and distinct physical regimes. Physical size, biological development, perception, camera magnification and computational resolution are independent. Organisms may contain ecosystems. Preserve identity, history, physical relationships and accounting across frame transitions. Reduced-resolution distant simulation must retain meaningful state. Growth requires architectural adaptation; similar geometry and density imply square-area and cubic-volume scaling. No claim of literal infinite recursion or fixed universal scale ratios.

UP-04 — Composition and organization of matter

Conventional chemistry coexists with bounded field-coupled and exotic chemistry. Composition, intrinsic properties, condition, organization, field interaction and history determine material behavior. Distinguish object identity from material lineage and composition; fragmentation, digestion, assembly and decomposition retain accountable matter within the applicable regime. Construction, metabolism, growth and repair share material architecture. Exotic constituents require a functional justification. No new element list, reaction table, registry crosswalk or material calibration approved.

UP-05 — Thermodynamics and entropy

Conventional thermodynamics governs ordinary processes; extended field storage, exchange and organization require explicit accounting. Distinguish total energy, available energy, capacity, power, conversion, distribution, expenditure and dissipation. Reserve is operational capacity, not necessarily total stored energy. Local restoration requires accounted resources or exchanges. Entropy is not a generic damage meter. Death and decomposition retain accountable matter and energy, with potentially surviving information.

Core performance clarification (user-approved direction)

Diesel versus race-engine analogies are engineering examples, not classes. Endurance race engines and high-performance diesels demonstrate that expected characteristics can overlap or reverse. Capacity, output, efficiency, response, endurance, recovery, load and thermal tolerance, operating range and adaptability are distinct. Performance emerges from radial placement, chemistry, geometry, phase architecture, energy distribution, environment, development and history. Evaluate operating envelopes against workload; do not assign permanent performance classes to Deep, Inner or Membrane. Preserve existing calibrated behavior until evidence supports change. No illustrative profile values or curves are approved runtime statistics.

UP-06 — Alternative biological architectures

Universal requirements for living systems: manage matter, energy, information and coordination. The original lineage retains rooted 3→6→12 geometry (one, two and four phases per fundamental function). Other organisms may solve the same requirements through different geometries, distributed centers or networks. Greater complexity or phase count is not inherently superior. Derive geometry from function. Distinguish adaptation, evolution and engineering. Alternative phase counts and player lineage alteration are possibilities, not approved mechanics.

UP-07 — Time, causality and probability

Conventional chronology, relativity and causality remain foundational. Distinguish chronological duration, process rates, biological development, rhythm/phase, evolutionary time and relativistic proper time. Shared-field extensions require explicit conditions, costs and limitations. Preserve accountable causal history; reconstruction moves forward rather than automatically erasing events. Deterministic, statistical and probabilistic models may coexist; condition-dependent probabilities and controlled seeds support repeatable tests. Quantum-inspired states, interference, coherence and other exotic extensions must be explicitly distinguished from real quantum behavior. No time reversal, timeline manipulation or macroscopic quantum mechanic authorized.

Architecture review standard (proposed operationalization)

For every future system identify physical identity and reference frame; composition; organization; energy source and destination; information encoding; forces and fields; dominant scale regime; clocks and rates; conservation boundary; causal event; retained history; authoritative runtime owner. Distinguish conventional law, BLOB-specific law, approximation, normalized gameplay index and presentation. Specify units, calibration, uncertainty and numerical tolerances before claiming physical equivalence. Preserve locked systems, independent tests, exact baseline provenance and integration gates.

Open decisions and implementation boundary

Field name and mathematics; calibrated units and reference scales; material masses, radii and reaction properties; field-energy accounting; thermal coefficients; alternative biological designs; temporal equations and probability distributions remain unresolved. Wave 08 numerical foundation and contact calibration evidence must be reviewed before runtime admission. This blueprint does not supersede existing implementation contracts, renumber checkpoints, retune Core reserve or authorize new mechanics. Reconcile with existing source documents in a later explicit review; no repository or project source changes made by creating this standalone reference.

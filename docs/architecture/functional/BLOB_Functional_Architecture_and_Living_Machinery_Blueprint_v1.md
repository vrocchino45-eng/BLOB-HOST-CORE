## FA01 — Functional hierarchy

Constituent → material → component → assembly → machine → organ → organism → ecosystem describes organization. A constituent participates in composition. A material has defined state, lineage and property witnesses. A component has a boundary and usable interfaces. An assembly retains the identities and connections of parts. A machine performs measurable work. An organ integrates functions with living regulation and maintenance. An organism coordinates itself, and an ecosystem contains interacting organisms and environments. One object can participate in several levels. Physical dimensions, observation distance, camera magnification and computational resolution retain separate definitions.

## FA02 — Component classification

A component can provide mechanical, Flow, chemical and information functions simultaneously. Its record would bind identity, composition, geometry, boundary/frame, interfaces, capacity, condition, ownership and history. Distinguish material-intrinsic properties, component-effective properties, and contact/interface properties that depend on the counterparty and state. A part’s authored function gives eligible behavior and an operating envelope. Its presence gives no output claim. Future material props reuse the existing material and chemistry owners. ## FA03 — Connection grammar

| State | Required witness |
| --- | --- |
| Proximity | Endpoint distance in a named common frame. |
| Contact | Authoritative submitted geometry, matched stamp and admissible contact; a visible overlap is insufficient. |
| Attachment | A sustained mechanical relationship with a support/constraint owner and release conditions. |
| Functional connection | An admitted channel and compatible endpoints with a capacity and boundary. |
| Activation | Accepted current/work/transfer under a valid operating condition with explicit source and sink. |
| Integration | The coordinated assembly produces a useful, attributable consequence and maintains its state. |

Mechanical, Flow, chemical and information channels have individual interface and failure states. Each relationship retains endpoint identity and generations, direction, capacity, condition, validity interval, and history. Geometry describes where a connection is; topology describes what connects. Proximity does not grant contact, contact does not grant attachment, and an attached object may have no enabled transmission. Native tendril attachment, authenticated two-grain contact and Shelf current demonstrate separate mechanisms. General attachment, generalized transmission graphs and assembly are future admissions.

## FA05 — Anatomical machinery

Silt, Vascular, Grave, Hub, Membrane, Nursery and Aperture are nonexclusive specializations. They can express settling/matter exchange, rhythm and circulation, retained history, coordination or travel, boundary protection, growth, or selective exposure while participating in multiple functions. Matter circulation, energy circulation and information coordination depend on one another but retain separate accounting. Organ development requires functional integration, continued maintenance, regulation and adaptation. The authored Host defines origin, condition and exposures. The player can develop a different viable arrangement from that Host. These descriptions organize design; locally scripted Silt, Vascular and Grave effects are active, and general living machinery remains proposed.

## FA07 — Persistence and degradation

Track identity, lineage, composition, organization, location, condition, relationships and history separately. Attachment retains component identities. Assembly leaves constituent inventory accountable exactly once. Fragmentation and reconstruction must retain provenance without duplicating matter. Repair restores a defined existing function. Regeneration regrows a specified structure with accounted material. Adaptation changes functional organization in response to condition or history. Keep physical scale, simulation resolution, chronology, process rate and developmental stage independent. Restore credible state on remount without arbitrary resource duplication or offline punishment. No persistent construction, general repair budget, degradation clock or organism growth service is presently established.

A child should be able to play through BLOB using intuitive interaction and practical understanding. An engineer should be able to investigate, customize and optimize the same simulation in substantial depth. Geometry, color, motion, sound, rhythm and consequences teach what works. Ordinary progression requires no calculations or specialized terminology. Allow multiple viable solutions and optional analysis. Assistance and deliberate engineering obey the same rules and share state and provenance. Future acceptance tests pair intuitive action and consequence with expert observation, performance comparison and optimization, and compare the physical histories under matched inputs.

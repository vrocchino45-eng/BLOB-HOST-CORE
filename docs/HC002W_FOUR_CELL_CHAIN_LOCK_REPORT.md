This pass should prove that the new build direction is stable:

## What This Chain Currently Proves

- The player can begin inside a living membrane nursery pocket.
- The seed can be targeted, bonded with, and absorbed.
- Scar matter resists unsafe absorption.
- The first morph happens as tissue/perception change, not a door or teleport.
- Scar-Flow becomes a playable post-arrival cell.
- Scar-Flow has a small interaction loop.
- The second morph reaches Signal Chorus without a gate.
- Signal Chorus has arrival stabilization and an interaction loop.
- The third morph reaches Bloom Residue Fork.
- Bloom Residue has arrival stabilization and an interaction loop.
- Targeting is now routed through data-driven focus rules.
- Visual composition ownership is split into composer domains.
- Debug display can be compacted or hidden.

# HC-008B — Surface Wake Ecological Causality

## Mode

Surface Wake Level 2 gameplay, causality, identity, and presentation.

## Purpose

Make the established nursery progression feel caused by a living ecology instead of by a checklist of actions. The trigger certifies that the experience occurred; it is not the experience itself.

## Locked systems

| Moment | Existing owner | HC-008B authority |
|---|---|---|
| Listening Fold completion | SurfaceWakeLayeredInteractionSystem | Observe and visualize only |
| Protected-seed absorption | BondSystem | Present and contextualize only |
| First morph activation | LocalCellMorphSystem | Express and remember only |

## Runtime arc

| Phase | World condition | Existing cast response | Player meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Awaken | Quiet nursery breathing | Tender guards; Watcher dormant; Guide stays near | I arrived inside something alive |
| Observe | Presence or movement is noticed | Watcher tracks; Tender holds boundary; current cycles | I can read care, rhythm, and warning |
| Communicate | Fold receives and holds an answer | Signal and current carry response across nursery | Communication changes the whole place |
| Receive | Fold settles | Tender moves aside; Watcher attends seed; Guide interprets offer | Nourishment is presented, not taken blindly |
| Outgrow | Seed has been absorbed | Old circuit distorts; scar and signal reveal shared seam | Growth changes what the space can contain |
| Depart | Existing morph begins | Tender repairs; Watcher records expression | The world remembers how I left |

## Acceptance tests

- No new entity, route, region, collectible class, ability, or hard prerequisite exists.
- The three mote identities are distinct.
- The current exposes inhale, pressure, carry, and calm phases.
- Scar hardening recovers after Absorb is released.
- The Guide cannot lead to the protected seed before Listening Fold completion.
- Fold response, seed presentation, and exit seam are visible world events.
- Signal and scar departure expressions share the same established destination.
- F1 explains both progression state and produced experience.

## Regression tests

- All inherited first-slice trigger and ownership validations pass.
- Guide Threshold, Hub, Status Page, Grave availability, dormant routes, and Grave return remain unchanged.

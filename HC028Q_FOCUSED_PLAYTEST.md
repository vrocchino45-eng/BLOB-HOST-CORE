# HC-028Q Focused Playtest — Vascular Relay

## Main question

Does the Relay feel like a third kind of living thing that speaks the same ecology language, rather than a Tender/Watcher reskin or a route marker?

## Test

1. Start normally and confirm Tender/Watcher nursery behavior still looks familiar.
2. Travel Hub → Vascular. A single Relay should appear roughly 2.7 world units into the route relative to the Vascular entry position.
3. Before pressing anything, watch it for 10–15 seconds. It should face/ride the current, subtly yield its lane when you get close, and continue functioning without player attention.
4. **Pulse:** hold long enough for relationship memory, then release. Expected: downstream/forward relay character and an existing-Host-mote wake rather than an attack.
5. **Absorb:** expected: upstream cupping/current-intake character; nearby matter should gather/hold rather than read as the Relay being absorbed.
6. **Stabilize:** expected: vane-pair alignment/flow brace; less drift and more coherent local Host matter.
7. Try P+S, P+A, A+S, and all three. The differences can be subtle, but should read as transmission/recirculation/conduit states rather than Tender care or Watcher interrogation.
8. Stop interacting and wait for the immediate response to decay. A faint Relay residue should eventually remain at the interaction site for roughly 29–36 seconds depending on the retained mode.
9. If a bonded Tender is present in Vascular, observe any residue recognition/cross-influence. It must stay bounded and must not create a recursive residue loop.
10. Leave Vascular and re-enter. The Relay should re-anchor relative to the new route entry rather than remain at the previous world's entry position.

## Regression checks

- Tender bond/repair unchanged.
- Watcher remains nursery-anchored and does not suddenly appear in the historical signal-chorus reuse location.
- Hub deliberate route commits still work.
- Comb entry does not immediately eject the player; leave/re-enter test still works.
- No `recipe is not defined` startup crash.
- Relay cannot be absorbed, bonded, used as a route key, or farmed for a resource.
- No health bar, enemy wind-up, target lock, waypoint behavior, or required objective prompt appears.

## Acceptance feel

The target reaction is: **“That thing lives differently from the Tender and Watcher, but I understand the language it is speaking.”**

If it instead reads as a floating route marker, a blue pickup, a passive enemy, or just another sensor, do not expand ecology distribution yet; tune Q first.

# HC-028P Focused Playtest — Ecological Cross-Influence

## Goal
Confirm that one organism can visibly influence another **through Host residue**, without either organism behaving like it received a waypoint, objective, combat target or affinity reward.

## Test 1 — Tender residue → Watcher reply
1. In the nursery, create a meaningful Tender care/reciprocity interaction that eventually leaves an HC028N care residue.
2. Let the immediate Tender response fully end.
3. Stay where the Watcher can sense that residue.
4. Watch the Watcher rather than pressing more verbs.

Expected:
- HC028O body language first reads the Tender residue as a foreign care/coherent signature.
- HC028P then briefly tightens/holds a small Host-mote pocket around the Watcher (`foreign_signature_hold`).
- The Watcher remains anchored and does not travel toward the residue.
- The reply lasts only a few seconds even if the Tender residue remains nearby.

## Test 2 — Watcher residue → Tender reply
1. Produce a Watcher sample/counter-sense interaction and wait for its HC028N trace.
2. Let the Tender remain near enough to read it.
3. Stop input and observe.

Expected:
- Tender reads the sensor/counter residue cautiously.
- A short **protective listening hush** forms around the Tender: tighter, quieter, more damped local Host matter.
- Tender does not path toward the residue because of HC028P.

## Test 3 — No self-feedback
Leave a Tender care residue while the Tender itself remains near it.

Expected:
- HC028O may still show `familiar_care_trace` body recognition.
- HC028P must **not** create a cross-influence response from that self-trace.

Repeat with the Watcher's own sample residue.

## Test 4 — No endless loop
Keep an organism near the same other-organism residue for at least 10 seconds.

Expected:
- The cross-influence matter reply happens once and decays after roughly 4 seconds.
- It does not continually restart while the same recognition remains active.
- Move out of recognition range, then return. A fresh read may produce a fresh reply.

## Test 5 — Existing ecology remains dominant
Verify:
- Tender bond and repair still work as before.
- Watcher remains anchored.
- HC028N residues still stay at their original world positions and decay normally.
- Comb entry/re-entry remains functional.
- No new prompt, HUD meter, objective, resource or route gate appears.

## Perceptual question
The pass succeeds if, with no debug HUD, you can occasionally observe:

**one organism changed the Host → another organism noticed that old change → the second organism briefly changed the Host around itself**

It should feel like the world is beginning to converse with itself, not like two scripted NPCs exchanging signals.

# HC-031C Focused Playtest — Rupture Harvest V1

Target: **12–15 minutes**. Do not extend the test just to collect everything.

## Primary test

Reach Grave II normally. Confirm the octave/resonance space still reads exactly as the accepted HC031A1/HC031B space, then look for three new pressure-bearing objects distributed through it.

For each rupture family:

1. Establish a normal retained relationship.
2. Pulse it and watch whether the object becomes increasingly agitated without a health bar.
3. Before rupture, try Stabilize and confirm the pressure visibly settles.
4. Build pressure again until it ruptures.
5. Immediately use normal Absorb on the released pieces.
6. Do **not** deliberately wait around to clean every fragment. Let some escape.
7. Check whether missed material becomes quiet floating residue rather than simply vanishing.

## What matters most

- Does rupture feel like loss of material coherence rather than "enemy HP reached zero"?
- Is the burst itself readable without bringing back visual clutter?
- Do brittle / silt / echo rupture differently enough to feel materially distinct?
- Does Absorb naturally become a post-rupture harvest action?
- Is there useful tension in choosing which released pieces to chase?
- Does leftover residue make the room feel like the event actually happened?
- Can you always leave Grave II regardless of combat state?

## Failure conditions

Treat HC031C as failed if:

- rupture reads like a generic shooter explosion,
- intact objects can be swallowed whole before rupture,
- all fragments are trivially vacuumed at once,
- missed fragments disappear with no aftermath,
- fragments overwhelm targeting or screen readability,
- combat blocks the Grave exit,
- HC031B regional resonance becomes harder to read because combat VFX dominates it.

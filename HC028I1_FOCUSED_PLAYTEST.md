2. **Arrival check:** remain still for several seconds. You must stay in Comb; arrival must not throw you back to Hub.
3. Move forward/deeper into Comb past the first filter area. The return fold should now be armed invisibly by having genuinely departed the arrival seam.
4. Move back toward the authored entry/return fold. Crossing it should return you to Hub spatially with no button requirement.
5. In Hub, approach the Comb selector again and press A/E. You must be carried into Comb again.
6. Repeat steps 2–4 once more. The second visit must also have a working return fold.
7. Regression sample: re-enter Grave, Vascular, and Silt if convenient. Their return folds should also remain repeatable after revisits and must not fire at arrival unless the player has first departed their return seam.

## Candidate pair and presentation contract

A future pair record should bind both material identities/generations and current material condition to the same contact step and mount. Include occupied/proxy geometry, contact points and frame/normal, relative normal and tangential velocities, external load and medium witness, optional roughness/directional parameters, admitted restitution, bounded tangential law, source provenance and valid operating envelope. Define fail-closed behavior if required parameters are absent or invalid. Configuration can vary a sanctioned scalar without generalizing to chemistry families or borrowing the 1.10 Stabilize surface traction multiplier.

In ordinary framing, the material body silhouette should anchor actual occupied-space expectations. Keep field wakes, shell glint and motes visually distinct. Preserve the two grain identities and local motion direction. A restrained contact-local accent should correspond to committed owner contact and preserve identity when a body is partly occluded. Retain opt-in proxy outlines for calibration. A contact-normal-aligned view cannot guarantee two readable overlapping silhouettes, so verify closer/oblique views before a final art contract. No permanent resize, new camera, deletion, or disabling is recommended. Quarantine temporary replacement meshes as diagnostic evidence.

PI06 needs phase-specific witness geometry and a sanctioned contact pair with an independently measured tangential direction, not revised art. FL-N02 needs its own material-property owner, operating boundary, sender/receiver and explicit loss witness. Defer production changes until both prerequisites are reviewed.

## Material and network contracts

Candidate property families are conductive, resistive, capacitive, inductive, insulating, directional, field-coupled, resonant, switching, and regulatory. Intrinsic properties belong to a material and its state. Effective component properties depend additionally on geometry, orientation, organization, interface condition, frequency, and surrounding medium. A long and narrow conducting path can have more resistance than one made of the same material with a shorter and wider path. That geometry dependence requires an admitted homogeneous linear law before use. Field-storage characteristics and field coupling can inform future effective capacitance and inductance. The effective device limits and the material's tolerance need separate witnesses. No chemistry-family assignment or coefficient is registered here.

The design can accommodate continuous directional, alternating, pulsed directional, and modulated operation. Future network proposals may specify series, parallel, Star/Wye, Delta, hybrid, or distributed organization. Each must state port orientation, phase sequence, frequency, phase angle, synchronization, impedance model, resonance assumptions, and conversion or regulation costs. Balanced sinusoidal equal-impedance three-phase references with 120° spacing give Wye line voltage magnitude √3 times phase voltage and equal line/phase current magnitudes. Delta has equal line/phase voltage magnitudes and line current magnitude √3 times phase current. Harmonics, transient states, imbalance, coupled paths, or incoherent phase references require separate models. These relationships are conditional reference calculations and confer no phase behavior on the Core.

## Accounting boundaries

Use separate ledgers for mechanical, Flow, chemical, and stored field energy, with explicit external work and dissipative sinks. Operational reserve remains a bounded capability. Any conversion between domains needs an identified source, recipient, time integral, efficiency/loss, units, limits, and reversible or irreversible boundary. Inventory Q tracks matter. Information throughput and retained patterns track encoding validity and lifetime. Contact momentum loss, feedback strain, receipt validity and rendered circulation carry distinct meanings.

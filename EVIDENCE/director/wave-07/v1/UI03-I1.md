# UI03-I1 · Shared player information · isolated candidate

Based on [IN01](https://github.com/vrocchino45-eng/BLOB-HOST-CORE/commit/462f1278ea069b41b4c0216335f4bb9363ee38af). Consumes the UI02 phase separation, IA02 context ownership, and TA02 same-step witness constraints. The existing `PlaytestHudSystem.update` publishes one immutable `frameState.playerInformation` after existing gameplay owners resolve a substep. The existing reserve display consumes it with its legacy fallback. Other player-facing displays stay with their existing readers. No independent authority or persistence is introduced.

Five mounted UI03 tests, 245 maintained tests, 11 launch tests, nine contact tests, nine supplemental checks, and production build passed. LS1E1 and LS1E2 reproduced their registered baseline signatures. The two initial candidate regressions involved an unnecessary registry entry and an undefined-model fallback. The final patch fixes both without touching the registry or weakening the existing tests.

Headed Edge matched normal HUD panel visibility, number of elements, title, position, objective and reserve against IN01. Native F and G entered Inspection and Core Work. A fixture pose plus the standard gamepad adapter produced a same-step accepted Shelf current equal to the authoritative receipt, then cleared the live sample at release. Mounted fixtures also verified acquisition, directed launch, actual contact, retrieval, repeat contact and remount; those paths did not rely on an Experience judgment.

`MODIFIED_GAMEPLAY_INPUTS` is the expected independent Director gate while the presentation candidate differs from accepted rev.2’s source scope. Preserve that gate. An approved future integration must re-admit its fingerprint. No rollout or merge was performed.

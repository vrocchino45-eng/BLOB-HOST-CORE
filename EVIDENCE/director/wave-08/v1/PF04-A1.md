The 46 Silt motes in `SiltPressureRouteSystem.createSiltMotes` share one `IcosahedronGeometry` and use individual transparent materials. Their omission removes exactly 46 draws and may leave significant GPU variance when other frames are slower. The narrower test gives no repeatable timing gain and no evidence to optimize the Core shell or Flow anatomy. Further group-specific attribution under fewer probes and alternating same-session controls is the smallest next step. If that isolates a repeatable gain, prototype a presentation-only batch for these 46 motes. Preserve per-mote transforms, tint, opacity and sorted blending. Common geometry is already shared, and plain transparent instancing can change depth ordering. Prefer reuse of existing material or geometry bookkeeping only when it measures as a bottleneck. Test identical screenshots and same-step gameplay projections before any rollout.

No permanent quality reduction, visibility policy, gameplay rule or simulation timing changed. The matched snapshots cover Core pose, scope, timing, aim, attachment, receiver result, reserve, inventory, progression and receipt count in the timed probes. They do not compare different wall-clock histories byte-for-byte.

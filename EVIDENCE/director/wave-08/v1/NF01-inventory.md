# NF01-A1 · IN01 numerical inventory · revision 1

Governing source [IN01-C1](https://github.com/vrocchino45-eng/BLOB-HOST-CORE/commit/462f1278ea069b41b4c0216335f4bb9363ee38af). This inventory records the owners and numerical meanings on the first Silt calibration path and adjoining Core, Host and movement systems. Ranges apply to the specific owner named. `L` denotes a gameplay world length unit, `T` a simulation second, `M` an inertial mass unit in the proposed contract, and `Q` one conserved inventory quantity.

Precision review. `SiltTrajectoryMemory` commonly reports to 4 decimals and PI03 uses contact epsilon `1e-7` for its existing sweep/separation logic, including mixed relative and distance comparisons. Directed release normal tolerance is 1e-6. Those are local algorithm tolerances. Keep full precision for physics and convert only in the observer. Do not copy legacy epsilon into a generic material policy.

# NF03-C1 · Reference calibration proposal · revision 1

| Reference | Existing witness | Proposed method and acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Grain | Stable Silt identities, eight conserved Q, native free trajectory cap 2.1 Lw/Ts | Record center, positions/velocities in the rigid Silt frame and independently authored experimental sphere radii. Record each identity, generation, release and custody. Preserve Q. Use 1 Mr and either 1 or 3 Mr in the isolated comparison. No workbook or general physical calibration exists. |
| Core and growth | 3→6→12 count, authored local rig, membrane shell and retained pieces | Measure the effect owner’s physical anchors and bounds with one locked arrangement. Visual shell/pieces and Near/Far camera are independent references. Existing animations never establish mass, pressure or radius. |
| Pulse | Held displacement 0.28 Lw in the known fixture, native release increment, launch velocity plus caps | Record actual same-step accepted displacement, outgoing velocity and pre/post native forces. Pair fixture uses incoming 1:0 as an isolated speed ratio. Do not change Pulse or debit reserve. |
| Tendril | Authored Core-local root/facing and sealed world anchors, support plane/traction | Record anchor separation in Lw, current frames and actual support authority. No conversion of chord response or load into physical force. |
| Flow | Silt Porosity Shelf patch, 1.15 Lw reach, alignment 0.42, dwell 0.06 s | Use existing F1 position/action/receiver result as an index reference. Report actual current, pressure, resistance and settlement. No transfer or SI calibration. |
| Medium | Normed regional viscosity and existing Silt linear/exponential decay | Record law and rate in local compatible coordinates across a known duration. Physical fluid density, moisture, buoyancy and viscosity remain uncalibrated. |
| Surface | D11B fixed PI03 ellipsoid and separate F1 patch | Preserve the authored PI03 geometry and kinematics. Measure an exclusion corridor; reject ambiguous simultaneous hits in first pair experiment. |
| Time | Raw elapsed, admitted substeps, dropped elapsed and Silt microsteps | Compare fixed and catch-up schedules with identical admitted time and control history. Record actual fractions and owner stamps. External/native forces get their own accounting channel. |

Approval required for use in a runtime candidate. Accept the name and scope of Lw/Mr/Ts, admit the measured pair and actual experimental radii/masses, approve geometry and eligibility, and approve no-result tie handling including a numerical ambiguity window. Need no SI factors to test dimensionally consistent behavior in local units. Molecular, generalized biological, astrophysical, and quantum-inspired implementations remain outside this pass.

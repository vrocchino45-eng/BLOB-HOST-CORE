# HC031G Focused Playtest

Use the existing **Shift+1** Host Contact Shelf setup. A 5–8 minute run is enough.

## Test 1 — no anchor, no target

Move into open space where no suction pad is attached. Hold **Stabilize + Pulse** and aim into empty space.

Expected read:

- the three free tendrils spread into a self-brace triad;
- the Pulse charge/read narrows forward rather than remaining purely radial;
- releasing Pulse visibly projects forward even with no retained target;
- no surface is required and no new prompt/button appears.

## Test 2 — free directional material focus

Remain unanchored and point toward a nearby ChemObject without intentionally retaining it first.

Expected read:

- the strongest object inside the forward cone receives the focused response;
- nearby material can still answer weakly as ambient inquiry;
- the targeting system does not hard-lock or cycle targets.

## Test 3 — surface-braced comparison

Attach to each authored shelf surface and use the exact same **Stabilize + Pulse** input.

Expected order of recoil support / focused transfer:

**scar brace > stable membrane > weak residue > free self-brace**

Soft tissue should feel compliant rather than simply weak: it accepts attachment well but yields more under load.

Expected visual read:

- already-sealed tendrils tighten instead of spawning a new ability rig;
- suction pads flatten slightly more during the projection;
- the Core still uses the same focused Pulse function it had in open space.

## Regression checks

Confirm ordinary Pulse still works without Stabilize. Confirm Stabilize alone still behaves normally. Confirm Absorb/Bond, tendril locomotion, Shift+1 shelf access, Grave II, and rupture/fragment capture are not altered.

## Acceptance question

Does it feel like **Stabilize organizes the Core into a focused projection**, with Host attachment acting as extra leverage rather than as a required firing point?

## Primary test — relationship route, no seed gate

1. Start in Surface Wake.
2. **Do not deliberately absorb the safe seed.** Leave it alone for the primary proof.
3. Attach to/crawl on authored Host support near the existing Tender/Watcher ecology.

   - hold **Stabilize** while surface-bound;
   - add **Pulse** or **Absorb** naturally if useful;

5. Release the gesture and watch the community / nearby Host matter answer.
6. Expected: the existing **Listening Fold becomes readable** and the Host presentation should turn attention toward it.

   - approach;
   - Pulse-read;
   - sustain Stabilize;

8. Ignore the seed again. Follow the newly readable **scar/signal pressure**.
9. Pulse the scar plate or signal thread.
10. Expected: the existing continuous Surface Wake → Guide Threshold morph begins while you retain control.
11. Keep moving through the morph until Guide Threshold becomes the active area.

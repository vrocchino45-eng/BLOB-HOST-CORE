# HC-028G Focused Playtest

## Goal
Test readability only. Do **not** consciously optimize around the visuals and do not play to the rhythm.

## 1 — Pulse contour test
In any area with several nearby chemistry objects:

1. Fire a quick Pulse.
2. Fire a charged Pulse if useful.
3. Watch the material that actually answers rather than the Core itself.

### Pass
- The outgoing Pulse no longer reads mainly as “a sphere blows up.”
- Answering objects briefly reveal their own wireframe/topological shape.
- The contour looks attached to the responding object, not like a HUD marker.
- The old contact/return timing is still understandable.

### Fail
- Wireframes persist too long or fill the screen.
- Contours appear on unrelated objects.
- Pulse feels weaker/stronger mechanically.
- The new contour hides the underlying object.

## 2 — Absorb test
Find a normal absorbable target and hold Absorb.

### Pass
- Transfer clearly reads target → Core.
- Helical filaments and fragments feel like controlled incorporation rather than generic suction.
- You can see where matter comes from and where it is going.
- Existing absorb timing/strength feels unchanged.

### Fail
- Filaments look like a laser/weapon beam.
- The helix is visually noisy enough to obscure the target.
- Fragments appear to orbit forever rather than enter the Core.

## 3 — Stabilize test
Use Stabilize on several different targets/route contexts.

### Pass
- Initial state visibly wobbles/precesses.
- Continued hold visibly organizes the bands toward coherence.
- It reads as gyroscopic regulation rather than three decorative rings.
- Existing Stabilize gameplay timing remains unchanged.

### Fail
- Bands imply a fixed top/bottom orientation.
- The lock happens instantly instead of progressively.
- The field becomes a large target reticle.

## 4 — Vertical-force / “thermal” readability
Use an area where rising has previously felt inconsistent or resistant. Try to rise normally, then move laterally/deeper/along the route and try again.

### What HC028G is showing
There is no new thermal-spot mechanic in this build. The local streamlines are reading the **actual environmental force and pressure already being applied**.

### Pass
- When environmental force supports rise, local streamlines/beads visibly climb/straighten.
- When pressure/downwash is fighting rise, the field looks compressed, curled or downward-biased.
- Moving into a different authored current/pressure state changes the visual read with the physical feel.
- A resistant rise feels like “the Host is pushing here,” not “my vertical control failed.”

### Fail
- The visual says “up” while the actual local force is strongly down.
- Streamlines are equally prominent everywhere and become wallpaper.
- The cue behaves like an objective arrow or explicit waypoint.
- Movement itself feels changed from HC028F1.

## Regression sweep
Confirm:

- Hub: approach awake route → A/E → short guided flight → region control resumes.
- Pulse/Absorb/Stabilize strength and ranges feel unchanged.
- Vascular/Silt/Grave/Comb/Aperture route logic behaves as before.
- Core 3→6→12, Core Work, controller mapping and regional rhythm remain intact.
- No new beat-timing requirement appears.

## Main question
**Can you now read what the verb is doing and what the surrounding vertical force is doing without looking at the HUD?**

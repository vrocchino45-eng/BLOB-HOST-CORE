# HC-028E Focused Playtest — Regional Composition Triad

Play normally. Do **not** count beats and do not try to synchronize inputs.

## Test 1 — Vascular baseline
Run enough Vascular to recognize the established transmission pulse.

Pass if:
- it still feels like the HC028D2 Vascular baseline,
- 4/4 transmission/catch/receive/settle remains readable,
- movement is not more coercive than before.

## Test 2 — Silt identity
Enter Silt and use the route normally.

Listen/feel for:
- a slower, lower center than Vascular,
- a three-part gather → brace → release/read cadence,
- pressure/compression feeling like it breathes with the area rather than like a metronome.

Fail if:
- you feel pressured to time Pulse/Absorb/Stabilize to beats,
- movement strength changes with the meter,
- Silt merely sounds like slower Vascular.

## Test 3 — Grave identity
Enter Grave and play the existing read-before-transfer loop normally.

Listen/feel for:
- four broad compound groups inside one Host breath,
- quiet inner echoes rather than twelve equally loud ticks,
- hold → draw → return → settle as a heavier memory cadence.

Fail if:
- 12/8 sounds busy or "music-game" obvious,
- the low voice muddies Core/verb readability,
- memory/recoil gameplay behaves differently from HC028D2.

## Test 4 — Core↔World continuity
Across all three areas, watch the Core without relying on the HUD.

Pass if:
- the same Core looks capable of settling into three different environmental sentences,
- Pulse/Absorb/Stabilize still read as the same universal functions,
- regional difference comes from organization and feel rather than different controls.

## Primary acceptance question
**Do Vascular, Silt and Grave now feel like three different places speaking the same underlying language?**

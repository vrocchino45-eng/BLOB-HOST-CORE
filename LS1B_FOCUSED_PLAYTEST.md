# LS1B — Silt living material cycle playtest

**Goal:** Prove a repeatable cause→transport→sedimentation→reformation→return loop in the LS1A2 habitat, without resetting the original yellow packet or blocking traversal. Plan an **8–12-minute** focused session; the simulation is designed to keep cycling longer without requiring you to stay.

1. Enter Silt, visit the three existing habitats. Before absorbing the yellow porous packet, look for any new regenerated pieces: **none should yet be active**.
2. Complete the existing Silt pressure read (hold Stabilize in rib section, Pulse while held), brace, then select and absorb the **yellow porous packet** as usual. Original deep-return ability remains intact.
3. Look around the **Reservoir**: the bounded reservoir stock becomes active and begins releasing individual material bodies. Each one is a retained unit, *not* a reskin of the quest packet.
4. Follow a release towards **Circulation Veins**, then watch material continue to the **Sediment Garden**, where at least two deposits produce pale-green budding forms. The material takes time to travel and accumulate; this is not an instant scripted reward.
5. Pulse in the Reservoir or Veins to increase seep speed temporarily. Hold Stabilize in the Garden to hasten the formation of already-deposited matter. Neither is necessary for the cycle to run.
6. After buds mature, follow the returning units; the visible reservoir stock strengthens as matter comes back. Try leaving and revisiting Silt. New material must not reset to identical startup pieces, and the original packet must remain absorbed.
7. Look out for geometry intersections, material too small to see, pieces appearing to teleport rather than following seep paths, or disruptive effects covering the Core.

**Report especially:** can you understand which matter came from where, can you see it gather/grow and return, and does waiting or experimenting feel rewarding? A screenshot may establish appearance, but a short video is better for motion and timing.

**Not in LS1B:** independent organism harvesters, physical granular collision/erosion, freely absorbing each new fragment, cross-region matter flow, or save/load to disk. World state persists during the running session and unmount/remount only; inactive Silt simulation is frozen. This is an initial closed-loop ecology proof, not the full 20-minute sandbox.

## Validation limits

- These policies call the owner pure functions with synthetic local sites and fixed step time; there is no Three.js viewport, spatial pathfinding, environmental obstacle, browser Gamepad event, actual route progression, graphics inspection or audible feedback.
- The backtracker policy pauses and resumes a retained state object, but **does not** test real route unmount/remount.
- These numeric outcomes are sanity checks for the implementation; their totals are **not** measured player engagement, game-play time, or performance/physics FPS.
- The current 3/6/12 phase engine is a sequencer and progression consumer with debug override; no automatic topology acceptance or real phase-specific force is proven.
- A separate BOT1 real-input harness is required before claiming successful autonomous world exploration.

# HC031I1 — Focused Playtest

1. Start the build and press **Shift+1**. The Core should land just above a broad teal membrane, not in empty space.
2. Move **camera-forward**. The material order should be membrane, scar, then residue.
3. On membrane, establish contact and move. Look for a bright teal ring travelling outward with a visible soft swell.
4. On the red scar structure, use **Stabilize + Pulse**. Look for three orange bands tightening around the scar while it visibly braces/compresses.
5. Move onto the tan residue. It should spread, flatten, and sink under traversal or braced recoil.
6. While attached on residue, hold **Stabilize**. The amber six-sided cue should tighten/lift, then loosen after release.
7. Continue normal play briefly. Controls, free movement, targeting, verbs, routes, and progression should behave exactly as before.
8. Press **Shift+T** to export telemetry. A successful focused run should include non-`none` ecology surface/role values, a `hostContactVisualCue01` above zero, and grammar values matching what was visible.

Failure means any one of these events is only inferable from telemetry, Shift+1 points away from the ecology, or ordinary play behavior changes.

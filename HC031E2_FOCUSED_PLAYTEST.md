# HC031E2 Focused Playtest — Unified Tendril Language

A 3–5 minute visual test is enough.

## Check these transitions

1. Move through open space and look at the three trailing appendages.
2. Approach a surface slowly until one contact begins to engage.
3. Continue into a full three-point attachment.
4. Move enough to cause one planted tendril to peel/replant.
5. Pull away into open space again.

## Acceptance questions

- Do the open-space appendages now look like relaxed versions of the surface tendrils rather than blue exhaust/ribbons?
- Do their folded tips look plausibly capable of becoming the planted suction pads?
- During approach, does the limb count feel like three tendrils changing state rather than three free tendrils plus three new contact limbs?
- Does release feel like the same anatomy returning to drift?
- Did movement feel unchanged from HC031E1?

If the anatomy reads as one continuous family, the visual identity can be locked before tendrils receive locomotion authority.

# HC028I Focused Playtest — Ecology Resonance Foundation

## Goal
Judge whether the existing organisms now feel like inhabitants of the same Host laws rather than decorative NPCs, without suggesting new combat or hidden interaction rules.

## Test A — Surface Wake / both organisms
Stay near the Tender and Watcher for at least one full Host breath. Ignore HUD/debug text.

Look for the regional phrase moving through their anatomy:
- Pulse phase should read outward/open.
- Absorb phase should read inward/gathering.
- Stabilize phase should read calmer/anchored.

The response should be visible but not synchronized like a dance or rhythm-game prompt.

## Test B — existing Pulse relationship
Use a gentle Pulse near each organism.

Expected:
- Existing EntitySystem reactions remain intact.
- HC028I makes those authored reactions easier to see through fronds/lights/sensing ring.
- No new reward, damage, route condition, or A prompt appears.

## Test C — bonded Tender cross-region proof
If the existing nursery-care bond is earned, leave Surface Wake with the Tender and visit at least two contrasting built regions, ideally Vascular and Silt or Grave.

Expected:
- Tender retains its identity and companion/care behavior.
- Its body presentation takes on the local regional phrase.
- Vascular should feel more transmitting/active; Silt more gathered/braced; Grave more held/retained through the existing composition timing.
- Tender does not alter route completion or guide navigation.

## Test D — Core/world relation
In one region, observe the Tender while the Core↔World relationship shifts between less-coherent and more-aligned states during normal play.

Expected:
- Entity entrainment becomes somewhat clearer when the Core/world relationship is coherent.
- It must not look like an entity buff/debuff icon or success meter.

## Regression checks
- Watcher stays nursery-bound.
- Tender bonding condition is unchanged.
- Tender repair amount/rate is unchanged.
- Entity roots do not teleport or acquire new navigation behavior.
- Player movement and verbs feel identical to HC028H2.
- HC028F1 Hub A-commit flow still works.
- HC028G verb/lift visuals remain intact.
- HC028H/H1/H2 Core topology behavior remains intact.

## Pass condition
The pass succeeds if the Tender/Watcher feel like they are **listening to and physically inhabiting the same world sentence as the Core**, while the player never mistakes HC028I for a new enemy, combo, timing gate, or progression mechanic.

# HC-031L Focused Playtest — Movement + Verb Physical Grammar

Keep this test focused on whether the three verbs now feel like one physical language.

## Surface sequence
1. Land and crawl normally without holding a verb.
2. Hold **Stabilize** while attached; move laterally and across a seam.
   - Expect: firmer/braced response to Host drift, slightly more controllable traction.
   - Do not expect: stronger adhesion, harder detachment, or reduced stick input.
3. While still Stabilizing, add **Pulse** and deliberately peel away.
   - Expect: the normal deliberate peel still decides release; Pulse may give the departure a small coherent outward reinforcement.
4. Re-land and hold **Absorb** near a target/surface.
   - Expect: the target draw can continue, but Absorb must not silently attach the Core or prevent release.

## Free-space sequence
1. Establish a target relationship and hold **Stabilize** while moving around it.
   - Expect: slightly less environmental drift while your movement input remains fully available.
2. Add **Pulse**: should read as controlled outward pressure through the held relationship.
3. Add **Absorb** instead: should read as controlled inward draw through the held relationship.
4. Try Pulse + Absorb, then all three: should continue reading as tension/equilibrium rather than a super-move.

# HC-031C2 Focused Playtest — Fragment Field Capture

Run only 5–8 minutes.

1. Enter Grave II and rupture one breakable.
2. Hold Absorb while looking through the fragment cloud.
3. Confirm one or two pieces visibly curve toward the Core without needing an exact lock.
4. Confirm a piece disappears only when it reaches the Core and the harvest count advances.
5. Sweep the camera across different debris and confirm the field follows your aim.
6. Release Absorb and confirm loose fragments resume drifting.
7. Let some pieces escape and confirm they still become persistent residue.

Primary acceptance question: **Can you now physically pull and consume at least some of the burst pieces immediately after rupture?**

# HC031T4 Focused Playtest — Gathering Ground Attention Handoff

Use this as a short focused run; there is no need for a long session.

1. Start HC031T4 and press **Shift+8**.
2. Complete Signal Plain / Membrane Ridge normally and finish all three Ridge contours.
3. After the third contour release, stop using verbs for a moment.
4. Turn the camera toward the large Gathering Ground body beyond the Ridge.
5. Sweep across the visible body, membranes, and rings. You should not need to locate one invisible center point.
6. Confirm the trace changes from `Target: none` to **`Target: gathering_ground_cluster`** simply from looking.
7. Confirm `Host link` becomes **`Gathering Ground · attention`** and the GG HUD includes **`ATTN`**.
8. Pulse the body 1–3 deliberate times. Confirm `OBS` increases.
9. Keep looking at the same body and hold Stabilize for about one second. Confirm `REL Y`.
10. Follow the bright released connective anatomy forward. Confirm movement remains continuous and no teleport/cell swap occurs.
11. Export telemetry with **Shift+T**.

## Stop-and-export conditions

Export immediately if any of these happen:

- the large body is plainly centered but `Target` remains `none`;
- `Target` becomes Gathering Ground but Pulse never increments `OBS`;
- `OBS` increments but Stabilize never reaches `REL Y`;
- the route is still physically clamped after `OPEN`;
- attention jumps to an unrelated local object while the Gathering Ground body is clearly more centered.

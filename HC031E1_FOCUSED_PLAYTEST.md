# HC031E1 Focused Playtest — Suction Contact Readability

Keep this short: about 3–5 minutes. Do not evaluate traversal depth yet.

## Test
1. Approach an authored Host surface until the Core becomes attached.
2. Stop moving and look at the three contacts from an oblique angle.
3. Move slowly sideways and forward.
4. Reverse direction.
5. Pull away from the surface.

## What should now be obvious
- A sucker does not appear floating in space while a tendril is reaching.
- Attached pads visibly sit on the surface rather than hanging from the Core.
- Most attached tendrils are compact rather than long trailing strands.
- Tendrils are thicker where they emerge from the Core and narrower toward the sucker.
- Under stretch, the Core-side root elongates so tension reads through the whole connection.
- During a step, two contacts remain planted while one peels/reaches/reseals.
- During release, the tendon lifts first and the sucker visibly peels from the surface rather than popping away.

## Acceptance question
**Without the debug HUD, does a still frame immediately look like the Core is suctioned to the surface?**

If yes, the next pass can begin bounded tendril-driven surface locomotion. If no, keep locomotion locked and converge the contact silhouette again.

# HC-030B1 Focused Playtest — Visual Hierarchy Compression

## Test length

**12–15 minutes** is enough for this pass. Do not spend the run proving mechanics that HC030A/B already established.

## Primary question

> Does the target interaction now own the eye, with lines/rings/wireframes acting as support rather than competing foreground information?

## What to do

Use the same normal play behavior as HC030A/B. Deliberately include:

- a fresh relationship acquisition and at least one deliberate camera handoff,
- repeated Pulse on coherent and unstable material,
- sustained Absorb long enough to see fragments + funnel/filaments together,
- sustained Stabilize from unstable/strained toward coherent,
- LT+X, LT+RT, RT+X and full-system interaction,
- at least one dense scene where entity response, target state and Host geometry overlap.

## Watch for

### Good

- Core→target relationship remains obvious without looking like a laser line.
- Acquisition is readable, then settles quieter.
- Pulse's target reaction reads before its large contour cage.
- Absorb material motion reads before the straight axis/rings.
- Stabilize visibly settles the object while gyro/tether remain supportive.
- Combination states feel less visually stacked.
- You can see more Host/environment around the interaction.

### Too quiet

Mark it if:

- relationship direction becomes hard to reacquire,
- Pulse propagation disappears into the background,
- Absorb direction becomes ambiguous,
- Stabilize no longer reads as an active hold.

### Still too loud

Mark it if:

- the long relationship trace still dominates composition,
- Pulse wireframes obscure the target,
- three Stabilize rings still become the main object,
- multiple combined support effects cover the response personality.

## Regression checks

- targeting feels exactly like HC030B,
- P/A/S effects and compositions feel mechanically unchanged,
- coherent/strained/unstable/failing behavior still changes normally,
- material/entity personalities remain distinct,
- no camera/control/route regression.

## Pass condition

HC030B1 passes if you can still understand the relationship and active verb **while paying more attention to what the target itself is doing**.
